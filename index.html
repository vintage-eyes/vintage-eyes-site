03 / BEFORE YOU SET OFF

A little
good to know.

Still have a question?
Talk to us ↗

Which journey should I choose?

Choose the car tour for a single day without accommodation, Swift for a short Hampi stay, Heritage for more time in Hampi and Anegundi, or Regal for the wider heritage circuit.

Are these private tours?

Yes. These journeys use a private vehicle rather than a shared group departure. Tell us your group size so we can discuss a suitable vehicle.

What’s included in the price?

Swift, Heritage and Regal include a hotel, private cab and sightseeing. The day tour includes a car and driver, without a hotel. Confirm the exact inclusions, vehicle allowance, meals and additional fees with us before booking.

Can I customise my trip?

Yes. Share your dates, group size, preferred stay and the places you want to visit. We’ll discuss what fits into your itinerary and provide a personalised quote.

How do I book?

Start with a call or WhatsApp enquiry. We’ll confirm availability, the itinerary, pricing and booking terms with you. Sending an enquiry does not reserve a tour or take a payment.
