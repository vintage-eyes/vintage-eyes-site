01 / A SENSE OF PLACE

A little closer
to Pattadakal.

Pattadakal’s UNESCO-listed temple group reflects the Chalukyas’ architectural achievements in the seventh and eighth centuries. Northern and southern forms appear together, making this a rewarding place to compare temple shapes and details. Begin with the impressive Virupaksha Temple, then give the surrounding monuments their own attention. The interest lies in the group as much as in any single building.

02 / PLACES TO EXPLORE

Look a little closer.

01

Virupaksha Temple

A landmark of the temple group, built under Queen Lokamahadevi. Its carvings invite more than a passing glance.

02

Mallikarjuna Temple

Look at the temple’s form, then compare its details with the neighbouring monuments rather than seeing each in isolation.

03

Sangameshwara Temple

Include this shrine as you explore the group. Take a little time to notice the arrangement of its stone spaces.

04

Papanatha Temple

Another stop for appreciating the variety of architectural details found across Pattadakal’s monuments.

03 / PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Make room for the journey.

Arriving & getting around

Pattadakal is commonly visited by road from Badami or as part of a journey through Aihole. We can discuss the sequence around your arrival and overnight base.

Find your pace

Give yourself time to walk the group and look closely. If you’re especially interested in architecture, tell us before we plan the day so the visit reflects that interest.

Go a little further

Aihole and Badami give this visit a wider context. Add Hampi if you would like a longer Karnataka heritage journey with more than one overnight base.

Opening hours, access and entry charges can change. Confirm current arrangements when planning your visit.
