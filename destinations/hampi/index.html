01 / A SENSE OF PLACE

A little closer
to Hampi.

The former capital of the Vijayanagara Empire sits beside the Tungabhadra River. Its UNESCO-listed monuments stretch across a landscape of temples, royal structures and stone pavilions. Move between the sacred and royal areas, then leave a little room to simply look around. A doorway, a carved pillar or a distant hill can hold your attention as much as a celebrated landmark.

02 / PLACES TO EXPLORE

Look a little closer.

01

Vittala Temple & Stone Chariot

Begin with Hampi’s celebrated stone chariot, then look more closely at the temple’s carved halls and pillars.

02

Virupaksha Temple

An active place of worship beside Hampi Bazaar, with a prominent gateway that anchors the surrounding skyline.

03

Lotus Mahal & Elephant Stables

Explore a different side of Hampi through the arches, domes and spaces associated with its royal past.

04

Hemakuta Hill

Take in the temples and the wider boulder landscape. Leave time for the view, rather than treating it as another quick stop.

03 / PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Make room for the journey.

Arriving & getting around

Hosapete (Hospet) is a useful arrival point for Hampi. Share your train or bus details so we can discuss the onward transfer and your first sightseeing day.

Find your pace

Choose a short introduction or spread the sightseeing over more days. Tell us which places matter most and how much walking feels comfortable for your group.

Go a little further

For a longer journey, Hampi can be combined with Aihole, Pattadakal and Badami. Allow for the travel between places when choosing the length of your holiday.

Opening hours, access and entry charges can change. Confirm current arrangements when planning your visit.
