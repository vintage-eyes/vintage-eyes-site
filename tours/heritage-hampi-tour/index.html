06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you set off.

How is Heritage different from Swift?

Choose Heritage if you would like another day to explore. If you’re deciding between them, share your arrival and departure timings and we’ll help you compare the plans.

Is the hill visit suitable for everyone?

Tell us about any mobility needs or if you would prefer to avoid climbs. We’ll discuss the walking involved and agree on alternatives where needed.

Can we change the final day?

Tell us your interests and your departure time. We’ll discuss a revised plan and confirm any change to the quote before booking.

How do I confirm a booking?

Send an enquiry first. We’ll discuss availability, your itinerary and the final price, then share the payment and cancellation terms for you to review. An enquiry is not a confirmed reservation.
