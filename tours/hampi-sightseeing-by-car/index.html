06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you set off.

Is the price per person?

No. The starting price is per private car for up to four guests. Tell us your group size and vehicle preference for a confirmed quote.

Do we have to see every stop?

Tell us what interests you most and how much time you have. We’ll discuss a route that suits your day before confirming it.

Can we book a larger vehicle?

Tell us the number of adults and children, and any luggage you’ll carry. We’ll discuss suitable options and confirm the vehicle-specific rate with you.

How do I confirm a booking?

Send an enquiry first. We’ll discuss availability, your itinerary and the final price, then share the payment and cancellation terms for you to review. An enquiry is not a confirmed reservation.
