01 / WHAT TO EXPECT

A trip that feels like yours.

If you enjoy holidays that keep unfolding, this is a good place to begin. Regal brings several destinations into one journey, with travel between them as well as time on foot. Before you book, we’ll talk through the driving, the walking, your stay preferences, and your departure plans. It helps to arrive with a little flexibility: leave space for a rest when you need one, and tell us which places you’re most looking forward to.

06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you set off.

How much time will we spend driving?

Ask us for the driving plan with your quote. The route moves between destinations, so we’ll discuss travel time alongside the sightseeing and breaks.

Where should I book my return journey from?

Confirm your final drop-off with us first. Your quote will state the departure point and what transfer is covered.

Can we extend the trip?

Yes, ask us about the time you would like to add. We’ll discuss the revised route, accommodation and price before you decide.

How do I confirm a booking?

Send an enquiry first. We’ll discuss availability, your itinerary and the final price, then share the payment and cancellation terms for you to review. An enquiry is not a confirmed reservation.
